STATE: the mechanised soundness development is essentially complete. All 16
modules type-check clean under Agda 2.8.0. `preservation` is FULLY PROVED
(zero postulates in Proof/Preservation.agda), and all three forms of the
soundness theorem are proved: `soundness-fuel` (fuel-indexed),
`soundness-tree` (coinductive), and `soundness` (classical).

The ONLY remaining postulates are:
  1. `funext` (Core/Syntax.agda) — the standard function-extensionality
     axiom, not derivable in intensional type theory. Intentional.
  2. `tree-decides` (Proof/Soundness.agda) — the outcome tree is finite or
     infinite. The tree is a PATH (each node has at most one child, because
     `progress` returns a specific reduct), so this is exactly LPO on that
     path — a classical principle, not constructive.
  3. `never-reached→no-value` (Proof/Soundness.agda) — a never-reaching tree
     has no reachable value. This needs CONFLUENCE of `_⇝_`, which is NOT
     deterministic (`(λx. b) a` both β-reduces and reduces under the λ).

NEXT STEPS (in order of value):
  (a) Prove `never-reached→no-value` by proving CONFLUENCE of `_⇝_` on
      well-typed terms. This is a substantial but standard development
      (parallel reduction + diamond, or the Tait–Martin-Löf method). It would
      remove classical input 3 and make the `diverges` disjunct constructive
      given only LPO.
  (b) Replace `tree-decides` with a constructive alternative: since the tree
      is a path, "finite or infinite" is LPO. A constructive replacement is
      to keep the fuel-indexed / coinductive forms (already done) as the
      primary deliverables, which is what the development now does.
  (c) The other open items from docs/SOUNDNESS.md §8: erasure commutes with
      reduction (§8.3); η / proof irrelevance; the NbE evaluator refines the
      reduction relation.
