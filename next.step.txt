`never-reached→no-value` is GONE, along with the classical three-way
`soundness` that depended on it. It was not merely unproved: it is
REFUTABLE. The refutation:

  * `progress⇐ (⇐-lam p q) = inj₁ (canonical c-lam)` (Proof/Progress.agda)
    — a λ is ALWAYS reported as a `Value`, whatever its body does;
  * `_⇝_` reduces UNDER BINDERS (`ξ-lam-body`, Core/Reduction.agda).

So with `Ω = (λx. x x) (λx. x x)` (and `Ω ⇝ Ω` by β), the term
`tm-lam g d Ω` is a canonical `Value` that steps to itself forever. It is
`NeverReached`, yet it reaches a `Value` in zero steps. The postulate would
derive `⊥` from a consistent hypothesis.

Confluence cannot help: the counterexample is a value stepping to VALUES
forever, which is a property of `Value` (canonical/neutral), not of the
reduction's branching. (The shortcut "a value's reduct is a value" is not
refuted by β — the redex `tm-app (tm-lam …) a` is not a `Value` at all,
since `n-app` needs a NEUTRAL head and `tm-lam` is canonical — but it is
also not needed: `soundness-nf` is proved without it.)

The honest replacement, proved with zero postulates, replaces `Value` by the
strictly stronger NORMAL FORM (Proof/SoundnessConfluence.agda):

  * `NormalForm t` = `t` admits no reduction step ("finished evaluating");
  * `never-reached-not-nf` — a never-reaching tree is not a normal form.
    The TRUE form of the retired input: "no VALUE is reachable" is false;
    "no NORMAL FORM is reachable" is immediate from `NeverReached`'s step
    at every node;
  * `DivergesNF` / `never-reached→divergesNF` — positive divergence over
    normal forms, by guarded corecursion;
  * `soundness-nf` — the dichotomy `Reached ⊎ DivergesNF`, leaving
    `tree-decides` as the ONLY classical input.

An honest negative result, also recorded in that module: the tempting
strengthening "reaches a NORMAL-FORM value, or diverges" is NOT provable
from `soundness-tree`. A `Reached` leaf supplies only `Value v`, and
`progress⇐` at `v` returns `Value v` again on its `inj₁` branch — never
`NormalForm v`. `tm-lam g d Ω` is exactly such a `Value`. So the first
disjunct stays `Reached` as-is. The two disjuncts are also NOT mutually
exclusive (`tm-lam g d Ω` inhabits both); only `NormalForm` and `DivergesNF`
are.
